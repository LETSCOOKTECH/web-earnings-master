# Traffic and publisher unit economics

## Funnel
Searcher query -> ranking/AI citation -> actual click -> **human session** -> tool start -> success -> download -> optional return -> monetizable pageview -> revenue after cost.

Do not equate: search volume with clicks; top-site total visits with non-branded organic; AI citations with referrals; CPC with AdSense publisher click value; pageviews with ad impressions; reported estimated revenue with verified payouts.

## Illustrative formulas (not predictions)
- Organic clicks by query = estimated search volume × *assumed* CTR by achievable position × probability of actually attaining it (heavily uncertain); do not sum overlapping queries blindly.
- Monetized impressions = human pageviews × ad slots × measured fill (adjust for viewability/ad blockers/consent).
- Gross ad revenue = human pageviews / 1000 × measured page RPM, **not** keyword advertiser CPC.
- Net = verified ad revenue + other realized revenue − hosting/CDN − processing − bandwidth − tax/fees − labor/support (state excluded cost categories explicitly).
- Profit per completed task = net / verified completed tasks when comparable and reasonable.

## Required dashboards
Country, device, search engine, brand/nonbrand, conversion funnel, page-level value, return cohorts, valid vs bot sessions, CWV with ads, costs, verified revenue. Distinguish experiments from causal findings.
