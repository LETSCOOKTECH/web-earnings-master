# Search-demand and SERP market research SOP

## Required data
For every term: exact query, country, language, device, collection date, source, estimated search volume, organic difficulty and methodology, organic clicks potential, paid-search CPC as contextual advertiser signal, SERP features and current top URLs. **Never invent unavailable data.**

## Tools and correct interpretation
- Semrush Keyword Overview / Organic Rankings: estimated search volumes and *modeled* competitor traffic. KD% is a tool estimate, not Google's official ranking score.
- Google Trends: country/time/topic comparison scaled to 0–100, not monthly counts. Examine 5-year and 12-month windows; compare synonyms carefully and record exact categories/search type.
- Google Ads Keyword Planner: forecasts and paid advertising competition, not SEO difficulty.
- Google Search Console: our own actual Google search clicks/impressions, with anonymized/filtered query limits, attribution differences and average-position caveats.
- Bing Webmaster Tools: Bing indexing/search data and its AI citation/grounding reports when available.

## Opportunity decisions
Demand + task necessity + low attainable competition + original functional advantage + distribution route + marginal cost + retention + revenue viability. Avoid weighted scoring with invented missing fields: mark UNKNOWN and withhold final approval.

## Organic market validation
1. Batch 25–100 exact-intent keywords (not arbitrary mass pages) by job and country US/GB/CA/AU/IN; avoid branded volume leakage.
2. Inspect top-ten *pages*, referring domains, own content/tool quality, SERP ads and AI answer surfaces, brand strength and task fit.
3. Benchmark at least five competitors with actual sample tasks.
4. One task cluster -> one real tool page, unless distinct functions warrant another.
5. Estimate modest reachable sessions with explicit scenario formula, don't promise page rank or RPM.
6. Design an independent traffic channel before committing.
