# GEO — generative answer/search retrieval and visibility

**Trigger:** AI Overviews/Mode, GEO, LLM answer visibility, ChatGPT Search citations, Perplexity links, Copilot grounding, Claude Search, Gemini, agent references and AI referral outcomes.

**Scope & handoff:** Owns **cross-provider GEO tactical decisions, controlled citation experiments, source/provenance evidence and referral measurement**. `web-ai-retrieval-lab` owns retrieval theory and reference mechanisms; `web-ai-discovery` owns eligibility/bot access and web agent usability; `web-aeo-answer-optimization` owns answer clarity.

**Operation:** Reopen applicable primary-source documents at execution time. Source date and market/account matter. An observed surface or passing offline validation does not prove ranking, AI citations, store listing approval, installs, publisher eligibility or revenue.

## End-to-end workflow
1. Define the exact provider/surface: Google Search AI Mode vs Gemini App/API, ChatGPT Search vs OpenAI API web_search vs user-browsing, Bing/Copilot, Perplexity answer vs API, Claude Search/Claude-User, or independent regional engines. Do not pool models or claims.
2. Reopen official provider documentation and effective dates. Split search-index crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Bingbot, Googlebot) from training crawlers and user-triggered fetchers. Record WAF authenticated IP or reverse-DNS checks when documented; user-agent spoof is not verification.
3. Establish technical access: robots.txt, non-blocking CDN, indexability and snippet for Google, canonical HTML with meaningful content, valid HTTP status, no bot-specific cloaking, accessible interaction and functional output.
4. Check Google Search Console Search generative AI inclusion/inherited state if an authorized property is connected; worldwide rollout updated 2026-08-31, default included. Preserve explicit UNKNOWN when property/account unavailable. No toggle may be changed without user approval.
5. Produce unique reference-worthy material: experiments, original benchmarks, transparent methods, versioned changelogs, current primary sourced factual constraints, useful public documentation and actual tools. Do not assume increased page count or text 'chunks' gains inclusion.
6. Keep content semantically coherent and objectively verifiable. Present direct answers only when useful; clearly document supported inputs, quotas, privacy behavior, product limitations and up-to-date evidence. Ensure structured data matches visible truth; WebApplication schema isn't mandatory for AI citations.
7. Pre-register a realistic prompt panel across task intent, language, location, dates, specificity, comparative and follow-up prompts, plus clear negative controls. Capture engine variant, time, citation URLs, mention URLs, direct retrieval versus visible support, and exact answer verification.
8. Run independent repeated observations in authorized provider interfaces or permitted APIs. Do not claim to know hidden fan-out queries or weighting from sample replies. Never prompt-inject models to cite the site or fabricate community mentions.
9. Compute sampled **visible citation frequency with denominator** and a separate true human referral/task funnel. Ingest first-party GSC GenAI impressions, Bing AI Performance citations/topics where available, and separately attributed website sessions. Do not equate impressions, citations, model mentions, referrals, valid ad impressions or earnings.
10. Compare competitor references and counterexamples; identify observed content gaps, crawl problems, false claims, fragile browser controls and legitimate original evidence opportunities; test one change at a time and log uncertainty.
11. Handle independent provider opt-outs and controls; do not assume GPTBot controls ChatGPT Search or Google-Extended controls Search AI Mode. Respect account privacy, model-use preference and publisher/legal policy.
12. Revisit official announcements/effective dates on every consequential rollout. Maintain a verifiable citation log and alert only on real changes; no guaranteed inclusion, rank or income.

## Hard FAIL / UNKNOWN gates
- Google AI Mode supporting-link eligibility requires indexed+snippet-eligible; Google special llms.txt/schema and artificial chunking are not mandated.
- ChatGPT Search OAI-SearchBot is separate from GPTBot training and ChatGPT-User user fetch.
- Google AI citation, Bing grounding citation, Perplexity cited source, assistant mention and website referral are distinct observations.
- Experimental Chrome WebMCP/Lighthouse Agentic diagnostics do not provide search-ranking points.
- Do not count responses generated with different engines as comparable without normalization by prompt and time.

## Output contract
- Per-provider crawler/control and actual HTTP eligibility evidence
- Citable original evidence inventory and corrected misinformation ledger
- Prompt-panel protocol with sampled visible citation report and grounded controls
- GSC/Bing AI reporting and separately attributed human referral conversion data
- Experiments ranked by reproducible opportunity, limitations and safety review
