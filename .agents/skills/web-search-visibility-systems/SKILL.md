# web-search-visibility-systems

**Trigger:** Complete SEO/AEO/GEO architecture request, new-site organic growth strategy, metadata/structured data planning, SERP opportunity, AI discovery plus business outcomes.

**Read:** docs/foundations/06-seo-aeo-geo.md, 03-search-engine-internals.md, 05-ai-retrieval-agents.md and 09-source-refresh.md. Delegate narrow checks to existing web-technical-seo, web-content-and-image-seo, web-keyword-intent, web-ai-discovery and web-authority-distribution.

**Workflow:**
1. Start with a real user task, expected verifiable output, repeat rate, target country and language, not page-count goals.
2. Collect source-labeled queries and fresh SERP evidence by country/device/intent. Separate autocomplete, normalized Trends, third-party volume/KD, ad CPC, real first-party GSC and direct human traffic.
3. Check organic and AI result layouts: does user still have reason to click? Is brand navigational or problem actionable? Observe rivals' real task UX.
4. Specify one correct canonical task page per distinct job; expose accessible content and tested tool; ensure robots/indexability, accurate titles, headings, internal links, schema, media rights, proper statuses and privacy.
5. Build original evidence and ethical distribution where competitor weaknesses are reproduced; never use link schemes, fake ratings or content farms.
6. Validate with owned-site GSC/Bing and provider AI visibility reporting when available, plus session/task/revenue analytics. A rank or citation alone is not profit.
7. Prioritize the weakest observed funnel stage and a falsifiable change; define rollback and review.

**Output:** intent/SERP map, site-page architecture, technical and AI eligibility evidence, competitor-job comparison, metrics funnel and measured experiments.

**Anti-myth:** no mandatory llms.txt, no generic .com ranking boost, no fixed word counts or article thresholds, no guaranteed #1, no numeric E-E-A-T score. Current Google source has explicit AI SEO guidance; reopen before recommending changes.
