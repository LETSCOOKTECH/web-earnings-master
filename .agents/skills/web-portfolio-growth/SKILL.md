# Portfolio Growth

**Use when:** Review multiple websites with verifiable organic acquisition, returning task completion, policy safety, ad profitability and focus; decide whether to expand, repair or retire.

**Workflow contract:** Follow the next steps proportionally to the request. Prefer current primary sources to saved claims. Adapt the sequence when evidence suggests a better route; do not overbuild a minor task. Show exact verification, uncertainty, and safety boundaries. Read `AGENTS.md` and the matching `references/` files when carrying out substantial work.

## Portfolio operating cadence
1. Per domain monitor verified ownership, policy state, canonical URLs indexed, nonbrand clicks by major intent, task starts/completions, return usage, cost, adjusted ad revenue and net profit.
2. Reconcile model estimates with first-party Search Console/analytics and actual publisher reports; never assign revenue to a keyword from CPC alone.
3. Track operational risks: publisher restrictions, consent gaps, invalid traffic, CDN overages, file decoding bug, law/regulatory changes, security dependencies.
4. Decide: focus on winning tool, fix failing funnel, refactor architecture, expand adjacent distinct high-intent tools, or stop the site. Do not launch a new domain while the first one lacks working acquisition unless a concrete independent opportunity is verified.
5. Maintain research portfolio with actual marginal cost and opportunity cost; invest only where evidence suggests improved human outcomes and potential unit economics.
6. Update skills/source ledger for every meaningful finding; record change, reason and effect rather than permanent rigid rules.

## Artifacts
`templates/portfolio-weekly.csv`, `templates/portfolio-decision.md`, site-level monthly economics with confirmed/estimated/unknown classes and official policy review.
