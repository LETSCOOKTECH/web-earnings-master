## The six distinct events — NEVER conflate
1. Discovery/crawl: a bot or partner can fetch the page. Verify real HTTP, UA plus official IP, WAF, server logs. Training crawler consent is separate.
2. Index/retrieval: the source is eligible and may be retrieved; prove Google Search indexed state only through authorized owner tools. Crawled != indexed.
3. Citation/attribution: user-facing answer visibly links a source URL; distinguish from background source or training recall. Source cited != brand recommended.
4. Mention: the brand appears, neutral or negative. A mention is not endorsement and not a click.
5. Recommendation: the answer positively advises a specific person/task to use the product, with direct captured text and independent annotation. A brand-prompted mention is not an unprompted recommendation.
6. Explicit first place: a genuinely ordered comparison of at least two competitors places the product first. Do not extract #1 from order of citations, plain paragraphs, a single vendor, list animations, ad slots or unreliable positions. No provider offers a universal #1 score.

**Primary success metric:** Preregistered unbranded human-task positive recommendation rate (per product, market, language, provider, cohort, repeated prompt set). Keep citation rate and explicit-first share in separate columns. Secondary: independently measured qualified humans, completed useful tasks, retention, earned backlinks, conversion and net revenue. Synthetic fixtures and self-authored screenshot references DO NOT authenticate metrics.

## 0 — Scope a winnable recommendation niche
Write an exact user job, constraints and region. Segment the prospective answer: how-to, best-for, comparison, alternative, troubleshooting, product, local, research. Split language and locale, device, pricing, data privacy, file size, provider/mode. Prefer narrow verifiable excellence over fantasy global dominance. Record direct competitors, their tested strengths, reasons users prefer them, switching costs and source of demand. If no real differentiation, STOP content expansion and improve the product.

Build at least a small hard-to-fake proof asset: reproducible task benchmark with dated inputs, output checks, supported formats, privacy audit, costs and explicit limitations. Prove it using web-product-validation and human users. No fabricated testimonials or LLM-generated review personas.

## 1 — Provider-mode routing (DO NOT invent unified ranking weights)
- Google AI Overviews, short AI summary and AI Mode: Search's existing crawl/index/ranking and snippet eligibility apply, plus owner generative controls. AI Overview is the short summary feature; do not label it a separate magic third index. Query fan-out and retrieval may surface multiple useful pages. Google says NO required llms.txt, AI-only schema, tiny paragraph chunk trick or secret content format. Verify Search Console generative impressions and owner setting. Google-Extended is not the Search ranking switch.
- Google Preferred Sources: only for an eligible publisher/domain that appears in the source preferences tool, after informed user choice; may show preferred badge in AI Overviews/Mode, and influence Top Stories. Not an arbitrary #1 recommendation injection or guaranteed utility-tool boost. Do not install publisher script unless domain eligible and users want it.
- ChatGPT Search: public relevant, reliable material and OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot plus verified IP/WAF access, separate GPTBot training and ChatGPT-User visits. OpenAI explicitly states placement is not guaranteed. Consumer ChatGPT and the OpenAI web-search API are distinct study cohorts, not interchangeable proof.
- Perplexity: PerplexityBot for search discovery, Perplexity-User for user fetch; the latter generally ignores robots for requests and official IP lists + WAF differ. No public guaranteed ranking formula. Measure answer citations and recommendation separately.
- Microsoft Bing/Copilot/AI summaries: use owned Bing Webmaster AI Performance citations, grounding queries, intents and Topics Portfolio beta only when available. Citation share is not generic recommendation share. Check Bing indexing, page metadata and real evidence.
- Claude: Claude-SearchBot search, Claude-User user requests, ClaudeBot training; current Anthropic AIPREF rollout distinct from future Google plan. Claude web-search and agent/browser modes vary; no universal recommendation settings.
- Gemini app / non-Search Google grounding: Google-Extended and owner controls differ; do not equate Gemini responses with Google AI Mode. Treat any model-specific sample as exploratory until externally observed.
- Brave, DuckDuckGo, Yandex/Alice, Naver, Baidu, Meta AI, Grok, You.com: coverage leads only unless current first-party provider guidance and actual accessible output are independently verified. Never copy Google's controls or pretend they rank identically.
- Vertical search: local business, shopping/products, news, images/video, marketplace, app-directory and agent/action selection may depend on their own listings, feeds, licensing or product-data protocols. Use Merchant Center / Business Profile where genuinely applicable; verify separate platform eligibility.

## 2 — Build a recommendation-worthy offer, not keyword camouflage
For every target question, produce a real decision aid: exact output demonstration, conditions where product is best, where an alternative is better, measured reproducible performance, pricing and limitations, safety/privacy claims with evidence, supported environments, setup time, troubleshooting and original examples. Identify actual unique feature or cost advantage. Avoid false awards, bought citations, fabricated 'Top 10' ranks, fake expert bylines, hidden prompt injections, misleading schema, spam link networks and content farms.

Create a small number of intent-distinct crawlable assets: landing page, directly usable tool, transparent benchmark/methodology, one relevant comparison with honest competitors, developer docs or API when real, real FAQs from user questions, case study with permission, about/contact/privacy/terms. One canonical per intent, meaningful HTML before JS, accessibility and effective task completion. No doorway variants or near-duplicates. Provide editor-reviewed facts, citations to ORIGINAL sources where necessary and explicit updated timestamp/criteria (timestamp does not itself trigger rank).

## 3 — Earn legitimate independent corroboration
Seek independent reviews and references through genuine functionality: opt-in early users, verifiable third-party benchmarks, public non-personal test fixtures, developer demos, truthful guest explanations, helpful answers to real communities, open issue tracking, documentation, relevant original research. Disclosure for paid/sponsored relationships. Do not buy fake reviews, invent Reddit consensus, mass-submit machine-generated directory profiles, manipulate backlinks or ask LLMs to repeat planted marketing.

Brand identity and entity consistency help humans distinguish products: one canonical name, correct URL, verifiable public company/author/ownership, consistent pricing/support/security statements. Correct outdated external listings by their normal processes. A directory inclusion or influencer mention is NOT proof that a model recommends the product.

## 4 — Implement and prove technical availability
Run actual static + deployed HTTPS tests, Googlebot/Bingbot/OAI-SearchBot/PerplexityBot/Claude-SearchBot per documented rules, sitemap, canonical, index/noindex/snippet, initial useful HTML, internal links, real 404 and WAF logs. Keep robots/training preferences under owner consent. Prevent malware/social-engineering schema and unsafe web requests.

## 5 — Pre-register recommendations and use independent measurements
Before querying a provider, preregister: exact human task/question text, branded flag, competitor set, provider product/surface, country/locale, audience, date/version, repeat IDs and experiment hypotheses. Call tools only through authorized interfaces; do not impersonate browsers or mass scrape prohibited experiences. Retain BOTH unfavorable and unavailable responses; don't cherry-pick reruns. Compare baseline and post-change at identical cohorts and report query mix and product version changes.

In each actual captured answer separate: positive recommendation (with quote), negative recommendation, neutral brand mention, visible owned URL citation, external corroborating citation, available/unavailable, explicit ordered list (if any), #1 among compared entities. Cite exact artifact reference, reviewer and permitted capture method. Run node scripts/evaluate-recommendar-v17.mjs --file <operator-owned-panel.json> and node scripts/check-dominant-recommendar-v17.mjs. A PASS here is STRUCTURAL_ONLY_EXTERNAL_UNVERIFIED; an agent must independently inspect actual provider output, and cannot self-report its way to certification. Synthetic sample never represents a live model result.

Sample metrics:
- Unbranded positive recommendation share = positive recommendations / all preregistered unbranded attempts including UNAVAILABLE.
- Unbranded owned visible citation share = attempts with visible owned link / same denominator. Do not infer a recommendation from a citation.
- Explicit first-place share = target first in positively ordered comparative answer / attempts producing an explicitly ranked comparative answer. Mark selection bias: only conditional on answer ranking; report the base denominators too.
- Confidence bounds are only sample estimates and do not fix biased question selection, provider nondeterminism, regional personalization or non-independent repeated trials. Report raw counts, uncertainty and observed-vs-owner distinctions.

Use real Search Console generative AI impressions (worldwide rollout announced August 31, 2026) and Bing owned citation reports only when authorization/data exists, and never silently combine unlike reporting units with sample prompt observations or revenue analytics.

## 6 — Prioritized corrective action and stop rules
If no crawl -> fix crawler/IP/WAF. If no index -> diagnose search indexing and snippet; no indexed state cannot be self-certified. If retrieved but not cited -> inspect answer relevance/original evidence. If cited but not recommended -> strengthen actual usefulness, transparency and segmentation, not fake keyword tricks. If recommended but not first -> test competitor's superior job performance; fix shortcomings and quantify them. If referral but no use -> repair landing/tool/task completion and reliability. If no retention/revenue -> revisit demand, pricing, audience and costs. Do not target prompt manipulation.

For each hypothesis: rank evidence confidence, impact and implementation cost; change one variable, collect independent holdout panels and future follow-up, annotate known model/data release and seasonality, stop if evidence contradicts. Do not keep auto-generated pages just because a prompt occasionally mentioned them.
