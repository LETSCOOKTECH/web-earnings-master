# Authority Distribution

**Use when:** Earn legitimate human traffic, editorial backlinks, creator referrals, product-led adoption and AI-source visibility through real evidence and useful tools.

**Workflow contract:** Follow the next steps proportionally to the request. Prefer current primary sources to saved claims. Adapt the sequence when evidence suggests a better route; do not overbuild a minor task. Show exact verification, uncertainty, and safety boundaries. Read `AGENTS.md` and the matching `references/` files when carrying out substantial work.

## Distribution and authority workflow
1. Establish an original asset worth sharing: reproducible benchmark, useful open-source code, technical standard implementation, a diagnostic report, guide demonstrating a real fix, or a helpful embedding/integration.
2. Map audiences by where they already solve the job (specific communities, developer docs, teachers, creators, support forums). Do not blast irrelevant lists or buy links.
3. Publish one useful demonstration with before/after evidence, method and caveats. For videos, have a genuine working sample and make referral URL meaningful. Publish no fake claims.
4. Earn citations naturally: reach out to relevant editors/maintainers with concrete value and clear disclosure of relationship; don't demand followed links or trade links at scale. Respect community moderation.
5. Improve repeat use where plausible: saved local presets, batch jobs, templates, references, shareable output where privacy allows. Many tools are one-time; don't invent retention mechanisms that compromise utility.
6. Measure actual human referral visits and downstream task completion by source, not just mentions, backlinks, followers or AI citations. Track time/effort cost and whether channel scales.
7. Evaluate organic lifts cautiously: no assertion that one backlink “caused” ranking. Link schemes, hidden placements, fake traffic and spammy guest posts threaten Google eligibility.

## Deliverables
`templates/distribution-plan.md` and `templates/outreach-log.csv`, a linkable verified asset, experiment hypothesis and referral funnel results. Read Google spam policies before outreach.
