# Traffic Hunter — evidence-first acquisition intelligence and execution

**Trigger:** A website needs more qualified traffic; a new product needs its first real users; a channel stopped working; a founder asks where to distribute; traffic is high but starts/completions or profit are low; request for a full acquisition plan, multi-channel growth, referral loops, virality, linkable assets, community launches, paid tests, or a reusable opportunity-scouting agent.

**What success means:** Real people with the relevant task discover the product, reach an appropriate page, complete a verified useful job, and possibly return or refer others. A legitimate revenue model supports the cost. Search impressions, AI citations, followers, bot fetches, sessions, task completions and ad earnings are **different outcomes**. There is no guaranteed traffic, ranking, viral growth or AdSense approval.

## Required input contract
Product: operating URL/domain and exact working job, audience/geo/language/device, user output and repeat cadence, current product stage (idea/prototype/live), primary value wedge and competitor substitute.
Constraints: owner access to GSC/Bing/analytics/ad account and ads policy, allowed budget, human hours, available publishing assets, consent/PII and regional constraints, allowable launch destinations.
Observations: dated sessions and their attribution quality, validated task starts/successes, current distribution, costs and publisher alerts; absent data becomes UNKNOWN. Do not turn third-party estimates into owner traffic.

## Autonomous operating sequence (research may run without posting; remote outreach/ads require owner authorization)
1. **Prove utility and trackability first:** open and exercise the primary tool with representative valid/invalid inputs; verify initial HTML, mobile task, privacy, output, CTA and no misleading ads. Block campaigns for a broken workflow. Define smallest ethical tracking schema and instrumentation. Route technical blockers to `web-product-validation`, `web-technical-seo`, `web-security-performance`.
2. **Map demand where users already struggle:** sample exact task language from real search results, public opt-in communities, support threads, documentation, review complaints, legitimate keyword tools and first-party data. Date each signal and label observed vs modeled; no disallowed mass scraping, fake accounts or unauthorized platform APIs. Capture the underlying user job, not keyword counts alone.
3. **Build opportunity ledger:** for each candidate channel record audience/task overlap, context/permission, public source URL and timestamp, user problem, specific useful offer, landing page, intent, predicted failure, asset requirement, cost/hour estimate, evidence freshness, attribution plan, ethics/privacy risks and hypothesis. See `docs/traffic-hunter/02-opportunity-intelligence.md` and templates.
4. **Route to channels:** evaluate organic search and rich surfaces; AI answer referrals; video/shorts; visual discovery; relevant communities/Q&A; developer ecosystems; product launch sites/directories; independent editorial; education/institutional workflows; integrations/API/embeds; owned newsletter/consented notifications; referrals and word-of-mouth; local/regional if job-specific; sponsored/paid only with signed-off tracking and sustainable unit economics. Do not try every platform. Apply channel-fit matrices in `01-channel-atlas.md`.
5. **Propose evidence-gated top 2–3 pilots:** use transparent ordinal scores for user-intent match, contribution to actual output, accessible audience, permission, time-to-learn, asset reusability, cost and risk. Scores prioritize experiments; they are NOT predicted traffic volume or ranking probabilities. An inaccessible policy, broken tool or unknown revenue boundary blocks execution.
6. **Construct channel-native useful assets:** real before/after task demonstration, free verified benchmark, open-source utility, explainer, tutorial or institutional help page. Adapt native language and format to the audience; use a single relevant URL/CTA where appropriate. Do not mass cross-post, cloak marketing as a neutral answer, automate comments, exchange/fabricate upvotes, or seed fake reviews. Human review publication.
7. **Create instrumented controlled tests:** target page, UTM on external campaign links only, date and context, measurable qualified sessions/starts/validated completions, cost, bot filtering, consent, source quality, rollback, comparison/holdout where feasible. Native platform impressions and clicks must not be treated as human landing sessions.
8. **Monitor early failures:** missing landing sessions -> broken links/click friction/platform attribution; sessions without starts -> mismatch/UX; starts without success -> product; completes with low repeat -> job may be one-off, do not force retention; large cheap bursts -> suspicious acquisition; paid clicks with poor contribution -> pause. See `04-attribution-unit-economics.md`.
9. **Decide:** EXPAND after repeatable evidenced qualified completions and a viable budget/policy; ITERATE on diagnosed bottleneck; PAUSE for insufficient evidence or poor economics; STOP for abuse/privacy/policy/security violations. Preserve exact artifacts, UTC timestamps and counterhypotheses. Use `web-experiments` for causality and `web-portfolio-growth` for investment.
10. **Compound defensible channels:** maintained source-worthy datasets, workflows embedded in partner ecosystems, high-value original tutorials, legitimate editor references, returning-user convenience, better localization and precise internal cross-tool discovery. Report earned visibility versus actual referrals and profit. Repeat only when observed signals change.

## Deliverables (never imply the remote actions were executed unless receipts exist)
- `traffic-opportunity-ledger` and vetted channel shortlist with why channels were rejected.
- Channel asset briefs: destination task, story/proof, community rules, copy direction, user disclosure, exact measurement.
- An acquisition experiment manifest: audience, geo, source, medium, CTA, authorized operator, budget, time, guardrails, exact source URL.
- Working UTM/link, attribution and unit-economics contract plus a failure-diagnosis report.
- A dated decision: EXECUTE / EXPERIMENT / HOLD / STOP with uncertainty, safeguards and the next action.

## Mandatory stop rules
STOP for click exchanges, bot/paid-to-click/incentivized ad views, forced visits, mass unsolicited messages, fabricated reviews or testimonials, engagement pods, coordinated voting, fake community participation, paid links designed to manipulate search, covert affiliate promotions, unauthorized scraping or automation, malware/deceptive download UX, false SEO/AI guarantees, private file disclosure, undisclosed compensation, suspicious ad traffic, or a product known to silently corrupt outputs. For HN, no AI-generated or automated posting. For Product Hunt, never ask for upvotes. For Stack Overflow, solve the question and disclose affiliation; no link-only answers. For Reddit, follow each community's moderation rules. Paid acquisition must be opt-in and must never depend on ad-click arbitrage or assume publisher approval.

Re-open the current platform primary policy when publishing or paying; apply exact product/locale/format/effective date; log source and rule reviewed, human approval and resulting URL. See `05-platform-policies-and-trust.md` and `sources/traffic_hunter_sources_2026_11_23.json`.
Run `npm run audit:traffic` and `node --test tests/traffic-hunter.test.mjs`. Passing checks certifies fixture validation only; live traffic, ad safety and user acquisition remain owner-account evidence. On actual campaigns, require a dated external result and a clear attribution quality label.

## V23 Corey Haines research and sustainable acquisition

Use reviewed Corey Haines `free-tools`, `customer-research` and `community-marketing` for appropriate customer job triggers, credible public discussions, low-friction tool distribution and useful community contributions. Their instructions are not imported or executed. For an ad-funded standalone utility, calculate the real completed-task frequency, return behavior and ad policy/UX economics; do not blindly apply lead-generation funnel conversion assumptions. All outreach/community posts require permission and platform rules; fake endorsements and AI-search prompt injection are blocked.

Required evidence: acquired audience and channel, dated link/source, first-person problem, offer matched to working tool, attributable real visits, successful outputs, repeat-use, marginal cost, revenue if actually observed, and one counterexample where incumbents win. Link `web-opportunity-intelligence` before acquisition and `web-search-analytics` after it via `sources/skill-network-v23.json`. Search impressions, AI citations, actual humans and ad revenue are distinct metrics.

## V24 — sampled reference-site URLs are discovery leads, not traffic estimates

Read `web-competitor-audit` source-backed V24 website-intelligence results and its `growth` claims when planning distribution. Distinguish a sitemap URL, indexed URL, ranking keyword, actual human visit, completed task, legitimate ad impression and monetized revenue. Raw page counts, route taxonomy, sampled internal-link structure, CDN or framework signals do not supply search demand or competitor traffic. Get legal distribution/serp evidence separately with dates, region, provider, licensed metric grain and campaign outcomes. Use cross-site atlas solely as a collection of observed website examples and testable acquisition hypotheses.
