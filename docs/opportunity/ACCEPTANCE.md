## Live external acceptance (separate)
- A real, authorized Google Trends GUI session with exact filters and downloaded chart CSV or saved evidence.
- Permitted Semrush/Keyword Planner/Ahrefs research, with explicit modeled definitions and account permissions.
- Independent human problem sources that are original and legally accessed, plus disconfirming observations.
- Real current competitor output tests, not inferred from feature images.
- A small genuinely functioning output fixture, pilot and realistic operating cost.
- Human signoff before substantial model spend, business registration, paid API or product launch.

## Status separation
PASS_LOCAL = checked schema and synthetic fixtures. LIVE_EVIDENCE = genuine original sources and direct tests, not validated by these unit tests. GO is a **reviewed recommendation**; no autonomous account purchase or deployment. UNKNOWN_EXTERNAL is acceptable where data is unavailable, but cannot be converted into fabricated certainty.
