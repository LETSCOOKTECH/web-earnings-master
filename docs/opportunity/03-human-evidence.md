# 03 — Human problem evidence, public reviews and Reddit authenticity limits

## What "verified human" can and cannot mean
A Reddit post is public, observable material. It is **not proof of a unique, genuine, representative human customer**; accounts can be fake, AI-assisted, sponsored, coordinated, duplicated or outdated. The safe output is: "In a publicly observed thread, an account described X on date Y." If an original source cannot be opened, it is an unverified snippet. Independently tested competitor failure is stronger evidence of a real workflow problem than an unsourced claim about product quality, but still not market size.

## Legal access
Reddit's user agreement prohibits scraping without prior written consent and grants only conditional crawling in line with robots; commercial Data API use may require a separate agreement. Browser/computer use does not magically negate those terms. Use an authorized manual view or explicit licensed access and narrowly summarize public experiences; do not secretly bulk harvest, export user profiles or scrape behind CAPTCHA. GitHub public issue browsers and app-store review systems have their own scope/terms. If access is blocked, record it and choose a compliant alternate source.

## Sampling plan
1. Pre-register exact pain hypothesis and supporting/disconfirming search phrases.
2. Inspect permitted recent original posts/threads with publication dates, context, product/version, environment and concrete job. Identify repetitive low-value posts and source copying.
3. Include resolved posts, satisfied users, alternative workarounds and reports denying the proposed problem. A "positive/negative" study containing only frustration is selection-biased.
4. Cluster evidence by **independent originating conversation**, not number of replies. A viral repost or 20 comments in one thread may be one cluster; multiple unrelated independent problem accounts are more useful. Distinct reviewers are still not representative population samples.
5. Reproduce claims against current competitor output where lawful, using a controlled fixture. Record tested input, allowed data, version, result and limitation.
6. Identify *frequency* vs *severity*: one user who loses hours once can represent high urgency but not repeat use; a daily micro-task can have stronger return potential.
7. Run consented problem interviews if possible: last actual occurrence, workaround, effort/cost, existing tool used and willingness to complete a live pilot. Avoid the leading question "Would you love our AI tool?"
8. Triangulate with search volume estimate, SERP and real workflow constraints. Separate "problem exists" from "many people experience it", "can reach them", and "they will use or pay for our tool".

## Evidence classes
- PUBLIC_ACCOUNT_REPORTED: source view and exact report, identity unauthenticated, no volume inference.
- REPRODUCED_COMPETITOR_DEFECT: same-input tested current product, a real defect for those conditions.
- USER_INTERVIEW_CONSENTED: first-person task account, method and selection bias recorded.
- OWNER_SUPPORT_REQUEST: actual owned product/user support, anonymize and permission checked.
- USER_TASK_PILOT: voluntarily used and completed verified output; can support real usefulness for small cohort.
- OFFICIAL_WORKFLOW_REQUIREMENT: real institutional requirement but no volume claim.
- SEARCH_SNIPPET_ONLY: merely a lead; never an independently verified human report.
- REJECTED_SOURCE: bot signals, cross-post, planted claim, stale issue or unverifiable original.

## Record
Capture date, platform, source URL, original context, safe anonymized observation, task, user constraint, alternate solution, severity, frequency claim, cited version, risk of coordinated/fake posting, independent group ID, permission, whether claim independently tested, counterevidence and consent. Do not save author personal data, profile dossiers, emails, exact private files or unrestricted post archives.

## User interview prompts
"Tell me about the most recent time you tried X. Why did it matter? What did you use? Where did that solution fail? What did it cost you in time, money or trust? Can you demonstrate the problem with safe sample data? What happened after you tried an alternative?" Then ask for an actual small trial rather than a hypothetical positive opinion.

## Contradiction gate
If independent sources disagree, preserve both. Check country/time/product version and experiment reproducibility, and use targeted cheapest next test. Do not average a firsthand current fixture and an old rage comment into a fake numerical truth score. Reject opportunities manufactured solely from review mining.
