## 1 — Evidence hierarchy and claims extraction

For every uploaded video/transcript, keep the provided YouTube ID/URL, exact claim, timestamp if the source provides one, speaker's business incentive, implied product, country and date. Timestamp **UNKNOWN** when no timestamps supplied. A transcript may be mistranscribed (e.g., «सीटीआई» means CTR in context; «माइलस्टोन» is inaccurate for *mille*, thousand). Never silently normalize important numerical errors; retain an original quote fragment and a separate normalized interpretation.

Classify each statement as:
- **OFFICIAL_POLICY:** verify from live Google primary documentation and applicable jurisdiction/format. Supersedes old video tutorials.
- **PUBLIC_OBSERVATION:** externally inspectable feature, page or SERP, with date/device/country; cannot prove proprietary traffic or earnings.
- **SELF_REPORTED_RESULT:** dashboard screenshots, claimed $ earnings, site sale, Google account count, approval or revenue; mark **UNVERIFIED** without owner-authorized raw export and reconciliation.
- **THIRD_PARTY_MODEL:** Ahrefs/Similarweb DR, traffic estimates, competition or CPC; preserve *units, coverage, time window and uncertainty*; cannot represent actual analytics.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** suggested profitable niche, low difficulty, 100% approval, easy AI-generated tool or AI citation formula; require separate falsification.
- **POLICY_HAZARD:** coordinated ad clicks, fake visitors, proxy geolocation, click incentives, traffic exchange, disguised navigation, auto-refresh or evasion; **STOP**. Do not operationalize.

A video with a compelling income screenshot may have a true outcome for its owner and still be useless as a forecast. For a creator with a hosting referral, paid workshop, platform coupon, or software sponsor, disclose the possible conflict without assuming dishonesty.

## 3 — Research questions and field study design (no fabricated census)

### A. World market and age
Three denominators must remain separate: (1) registered domains, (2) hosting survey website counts including inactive sites, (3) actual active indexed AdSense publishers. There is no authoritative complete public list of publishers, rankings, revenue or exact 7-month/2-year age distribution. Page first-seen, domain registration, first archive capture and Search indexed date are different. Select a representative, stated **SERP sample** (country, language, date, niche, result depth, device) and measure proxy ages; publish numerator/denominator, missing data, bias and confidence. Do not extrapolate across all websites.

### B. Demand and advertising revenue
For 30–50 niche hypotheses, record real user job, search country/language, task completion requirements, alternative offered by Google/AI/spreadsheet, Trends relative index, Planner absolute range if authorized, third-party SEO modeled clicks only with provenance, and probable page monetizability. Reject if tool is unnecessary or an AI answer solves it with no click. Google Ads CPC may indicate advertiser economics; **never equate CPC with AdSense page RPM**.

### C. Competition
For each short list candidate: inspect actual SERP (search features, ads, Google-owned widget, AI generated tool, Reddit/forums, business directories); task-test >=3 genuine substitutes using identical NORMAL, MALFORMED and BOUNDARY fixtures; independently calculate expected outputs; record real counterexamples to assumed weaknesses. Measure mobile, accessibility, export fidelity, privacy, latency, price, language/localization, contact/ownership credibility, stale data and retention. If a superior free competitor exists, the default is **NO-GO**, unless measured unmet needs survive.

### D. AI displacement
Classify **ANSWER_ONLY**, **SINGLE_OPERATION**, **INTERACTIVE_COMPARISON**, **MULTISTEP_EXPORT**, **COMPLIANCE_CRITICAL**, **REPEAT_WORKFLOW**. Test relevant current Google AI search, public assistant, browser/OS/spreadsheet, and actual competitor with the same fixtures where authorized. Log task completion rate and click necessity, not a narrative “AI-proof” score.

### E. Operating economics
Assumptions must be labeled **HYPOTHETICAL** until owner's AdSense and billing receipts exist. Page RPM = estimated earnings / site pageviews x 1000; site pageviews != Search impressions, users, ad impressions or CTR. Model 3 scenarios, target country mix, traffic declines, account delays, cost inflation, zero-ad cases, support hours, validity adjustments and payout/tax friction. Paid acquisition used solely to inflate ads is forbidden; legitimate acquisition has to earn positive net value on its own verified basis.

## 4 — Seven mandatory handoffs to V32

1. MARKET_DEMAND: 30–50 actual sourced jobs then reduce to 3–5 credible candidates; no pretend 50 keyword tools already validated.
2. COMPETITOR_GAP: human operator runs dated SERP audits and 3 same-input product comparisons for each finalist, including strongest counterexample.
3. PRODUCT_ORACLE: issue a falsifiable PRD, independent reference output and real-browser/security/accessibility tests; code is not proof by screenshots.
4. SEARCH_DEPLOYMENT: owned domain, real HTTP first HTML, canonical/noindex/robots/sitemap, GSC inspection; crawl/index/rank/AI cite/click are **separate**.
5. REAL_DISTRIBUTION: sources produce genuine task completion; no paid-to-click, auto-surf, fake user personas or unnatural link manipulation.
6. ADSENSE_SITE_ACCOUNT: real user-owned Google Sites **Ready** evidence, original-value pages, publisher identity, live official policies, consent and ad placement review; never “approval guaranteed”.
7. RECONCILED_ECONOMICS: pageviews + actual AdSense report + adjustments + cash/fees + operating costs; no self-reported screenshot converted into verified income.

**Decision rule:** No build GO from documentation alone. All stages have independent verification; V32 JSON checker is a *structural* validator and cannot authenticate account exports or cause Google indexing.
