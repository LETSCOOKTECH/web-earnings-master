# Real market intelligence and independent competitor validation

First define the exact task: person, input, desired output, current substitute, device, budget, rights, frequency and country. A keyword alone is not a need.

Evidence ladder: original user issue/review/support report; consented observation; permitted real SERP and competitor workflow; current Trends/Keyword Planner/Semrush/Ahrefs data with actual units and scope; commentary/hypothesis. Deduplicate syndicated anecdotes. Write strongest disconfirming evidence alongside every favorable observation. Google Trends is relative sampled interest, not monthly searches, and Google Ads advertiser competition is not organic search difficulty. Never project visitor traffic directly from keyword volume.

For each significant rival or substitute, use one common permitted input with predetermined correct output. Capture its exact URL, source of values, timestamp, supported formats, size limits, privacy policy, actual produced bytes, error handling, pricing and user steps. Do not crawl or bypass protected areas without authorization. Include at least one better competitor and the real reason a user would switch.

Opportunity decisions are REJECT, PIVOT, RESEARCH_REQUIRED, CHEAP_TEST and GO_FOR_LOCAL_MVP. Even two dated sources and two rivals mean a candidate merits local exploration, not that it will rank or earn. Estimate reachability, CTR uncertainty, repeat demand, ongoing support and uncapped API liabilities. Avoid market opportunities premised on fake reviews, copied tools or scalable thin pages.

Deliver a brief with source IDs, original URL, scope, real metric unit, independent origin, contradicting evidence, common fixture and an explicit affordable falsification test. Human review is required to certify authenticity. Use web-opportunity-intelligence and web-competitor-audit rather than reinventing their fetches.
