# 01 — Channel atlas: map the user job to a real discovery surface

Each row is a **candidate route**, never a recommendation to publish everywhere. Priority is determined by task/user overlap, publication permission, measurable referral and meaningful on-site completion.

### Important distinctions
- Google Search rank ≠ Google Discover inclusion ≠ Google News eligibility ≠ AI citation; evaluate each separately.
- A YouTube watch is platform consumption; the user may not leave YouTube.
- Pinterest reports outbound clicks separately from Pin clicks. Use outbound metric plus landing sessions.
- Product Hunt prohibits asking for upvotes even if a launch is legitimate.
- HN disallows automated or AI-generated HN posts/comments; the tool may draft **private** talking points but the user writes HN posts personally.
- Community rules are venue-specific; no global Reddit '10%' rule universally supersedes subreddit moderation.
- Google AdSense prohibits invalid traffic; purchased legitimate advertising is not automatically disallowed but must be tracked and reviewed with current provider policy.
- For AI search, accurate citation is different from an actual user referral; user-agent and training/search permission separated by existing `web-ai-discovery`.

### Mapping jobs to channels
- **Document/photo tools:** exam/admin help guides, localized workflows, quick video demos, search, institution referrals, user recommendations. Stronger when output genuinely works under real submission constraints.
- **Developer tooling:** OSS libraries, maintained examples, GitHub readmes, docs integrations, HN/technical newsletters, and developer search. Fewer but often higher-intent qualified users.
- **Design/editing tools:** visual proof, creator demonstrations, plugin templates and YouTube/Pinterest discovery. Quality and content rights must be tested.
- **Business utilities:** professional-specific tutorials, community knowledge, newsletters, relevant software integrations and controlled paid demand pilots; ads may be poor monetization for low frequency.
- **Network diagnostics:** original transparent measurements, troubleshooting communities and independent explainers; do not exaggerate diagnostic accuracy or gather sensitive identifiers.

### Source priority
See source registry. Platform first-party rules control permissible participation. Use competitor reports and social anecdotes only as **signals to investigate**. Channel scores and pilot suggestions are project-level decisions, not external platform ranking weights.
