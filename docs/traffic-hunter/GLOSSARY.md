# Traffic Hunter — compact acquisition glossary

**Audience:** humans likely to need the exact user job, not total follower base.
**Channel:** external discovery/communication route, e.g. search, creator video, relevant community, directory, paid campaign.
**Surface:** specific product placement, e.g. YouTube recommendations versus YouTube Search.
**Demand signal:** dated observation that a need exists, not a guaranteed visit forecast.
**Intent match:** how directly a channel's users want the actual task and result.
**Qualified session:** ethically observed relevant human landing visit by an explicit project rule; not a bot.
**Referral:** a recorded visit associated with an external source; missing referrer does not prove Direct or AI origin.
**Outbound click:** source-platform event leading toward website; not automatically a session.
**Task start:** begin execution of the promoted tool; not page load.
**Validated completion:** output passes product correctness checks.
**Traffic quality:** legitimate users and useful interaction, distinct from ad clickthrough and vanity reach.
**Conversion rate:** rate with defined numerator, denominator, cohort and time; don't mix successes per session with unique converting sessions.
**Retention:** correctly defined return cohort, often inappropriate for one-off tasks.
**Acquisition cost:** attributable human/advertising/asset cost per verified acquired success or customer under explicit time grain.
**Ad RPM:** actual publisher ad earnings per thousand eligible pageviews, not advertiser CPC.
**Gross contribution:** attributable revenue less incremental campaign/product cost, not fully loaded profit.
**Dark/unknown attribution:** sources withheld by privacy, apps, browsers or redirects; not permission to guess.
**Owned/earned/paid/partner:** different distribution control and measurement classes.
**Editorial backlink:** independent publisher link earned for relevance; not paid link manipulation.
**Viral coefficient:** model of new qualified users referred per existing qualified user; not an automatically measured constant.
**Audience saturation:** diminishing returns when repeating similar content to same interested people.
**Seasonality:** time-dependent user demand; must be separated from campaign effects.
**Opportunity ledger:** testable collection of real problems, specific channels, constraints and proposed actions.
**Pilot:** bounded experiment with policy clearance, budget/time guardrails, output metric, owner approval.
**Source provenance:** first-party policy URL plus date, scope, currentness and observed evidence.
**Confidence:** source/method certainty, not a guessed percentage probability of becoming viral.
**Causal attribution:** evidence that an action contributed to a change; usually limited by seasonality, ranking updates and other campaigns.
**HOLD:** research or approval incomplete; no platform action permitted.
**STOP:** policy violation, deceptive claim, data leakage, unsafe user outcomes or unauthorized spend.
