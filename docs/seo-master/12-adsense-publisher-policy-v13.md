## Three different Google systems
- Google Search: indexing, crawling, helpful content, keyword stuffing, doorway/scaled low-value pages and misleading-functionality spam.
- Google Ads Keyword Planner: advertiser-oriented search history estimates, bidding and ad competition. Advertiser CPC does not equal publisher RPM; its competition is not Google's organic difficulty.
- AdSense: publisher account/sites review, genuine publisher-content value and rights, ad placement, restricted inventory, consent and invalid traffic. No fixed keyword density, article count, page word count, traffic threshold or "high CPC keyword" guarantees approval.

## Approval readiness
Confirm exact country/identity/publisher account eligibility, site ownership/control, usable publicly accessible domain, correct navigation/content, original useful working tools, content rights, appropriate privacy policy/contact and actual publisher site/account status. A site may be technically crawlable and contain a functional tool yet not satisfy publisher-content value. Check the owner AdSense Sites/Policy Center rather than inferring Ready from an ads.txt file or a local test.

## Page-specific ads policy
Home, tool and explanation pages each require individual review for original publisher content and actual task flow. Loading-only, 404/500, empty search result, under-construction, private upload/editor/result pages and near-content-free screens default to NO ADS until documented applicable rule is confirmed. Do not inject filler articles merely to wrap ad units.
Ads must not imitate file-download buttons, navigation, input controls, consent choices or calls to action; avoid forced clicks, auto-redirects, inadvertent close-spaced clicks, layout shifts and popunders that violate provider policies. Test layout without interacting with real ads. Do not click your own live ads, run test bots that generate ad impressions, purchase fake traffic or incentivize ad clicks.

## ads.txt, consent and privacy
Only publish an authorized real seller/publisher ID and correct root ads.txt. Text passing syntax doesn't mean an account was approved. Check page ad crawler and CDN/WAF when the publisher review requires it. Determine actual regional consent requirements, certified CMP/version where applicable, ad targeting and cookies, with owner/jurisdiction evidence. Do not invent legal compliance merely because a cookie banner exists. Never send private file contents or filenames to ad/analytics scripts.

## Revenue truth
Observe real human traffic, valid eligible impressions, viewability/fill, country, ad format, net publisher earnings including adjustments, page RPM, retention and CPU/egress/provider costs. Do not calculate publisher income from Google Ads auction CPC or copy estimates from third-party dashboards as actual owner revenue. A layout that earns more per view but destroys task completion may lose long-term value.

## Alternative networks
Check each network's **current official** publisher rules, content classes, prohibited ad formats, geo requirements, payments, consent and invalid traffic separately. "Instant monetization" is not guaranteed policy safety or profit. Avoid vendors whose placements undermine Search spam rules or primary task trust. Record country/account and effective date for every network decision.

## Output gates
READY_FOR_ACCOUNT_REVIEW only when actual deployed useful site, pages and policies have been inspected. HOLD if original policy 429, ownership/consent unclear, no real human job, no genuine publisher content or live site not yet verified. BLOCK when misleading ads, nonfunctional fake converter, invalid traffic or privacy/data rights fail. PUBLISHER_APPROVED requires verified owner account state; a self-declared JSON field cannot establish it.
