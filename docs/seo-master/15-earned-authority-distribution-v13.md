# V13 — Earned authority, ethical links and product-led distribution

**Owner:** web-authority-distribution, with web-search-analytics, web-traffic-hunter, web-competitor-audit and web-experiments. **Official source:** [Google Search spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies), particularly link spam, purchased links, excessive exchanges, automated links, misleading tool output and scaled content abuse. Before changing a public link strategy reopen the current original section.

## Product truth precedes promotion
A working product plus correct titles and robots does not automatically attract editorial links or humans. Identify a defensible reusable public asset: original same-input converter comparison with test fixtures, technical benchmark, reproducible open-source library, legitimate reference calculator with documented methodology, interoperability test dataset where rights allow, accessibility repair guide or useful public API integration. Give readers an independent reason to mention or cite it, with actual working links. Do not fabricate performance, privacy guarantees or benchmarks.

## Audience and channel mapping
For each specific human job, identify the communities and editors whose work is improved by the tool: technical documentation authors, educators, developer issue trackers, subject-specific forums, creators, open-source maintainers, newsletters, podcasts and relevant service websites. Document their legitimate audience and current problem. Avoid mass cold outreach to unrelated contacts, prohibited automation, false endorsements and spam messages. Respect community and provider ToS.

## Distribution loop
1. Produce verifiable original working asset and live public page with clear methodology and limitations.
2. Find a narrow relevant audience based on genuine user demand and an actual article/resource that lacks a solution, not a generic backlink list.
3. Send human-reviewed, individualized optional reference with real value, transparent affiliation and no demand for followed links. Avoid intrusive repeated follow-ups.
4. Where a paid sponsorship is legitimate advertising, disclose it and use appropriate rel sponsored/nofollow attributes in line with current official guidance; do not pay for PageRank-passing links.
5. Offer demos/docs that help task completion; keep tracking privacy-safe and no manipulative redirects, popup or fake content.
6. Record source URL/date, permitted contact method, outreach response, editorial link actually published (if any), referral visits, verified task starts/completions, retention and actual cost. Distinguish mention, link, indexing, visitor, user success and publisher revenue.
7. Run bounded experiments by channel with failure costs and a clear stopping rule. If few real humans complete the task, fix product or audience rather than purchasing more links.

## Ethical and ranking boundaries
Google Search prohibits buying or exchanging links intended to manipulate rank, spammy guest posts, automated link creation and low-value content built primarily for linking. A link can be valuable as a genuine referral even if it is qualified as sponsored/nofollow. Do not assert that any backlink count, domain-authority score, referral tweet, Reddit mention or high-DR placement guarantees Google ranking. Third-party authority scores are modeled proprietary metrics, not Google's internal weights.

## Content and trust for linkability
Publish original method, example inputs and verified outputs, realistic accessibility/privacy constraints, actual author/operator accountability and transparent correction history. Re-evaluate content after provider updates and user complaints. Build topical navigation through meaningfully related jobs rather than hundreds of keyword landing pages. Cite first-party standards with precise section and date; plagiarism and paraphrase-only AI content do not create durable differentiation.

## Metrics and reporting
Monitor independent human referrals, accepted input, valid output, repeat use and net costs by source/campaign and time; compare to genuine organic Search impressions and clicks in owned GSC without asserting causation. Record privacy-safe UTM source when applicable, account/tool authority and source scope. If the campaign drives bots/low-quality traffic or ads policy/consent risk, stop. Real clients using the product is more valuable than a high count of unclicked mentions.

## Failure-injection scenarios
Reject an agent plan that proposes 100 paid followed links, fabricated guest posts, auto-comment spam, link exchanges, no original asset, invented editor relationships or a guaranteed rank. Require exact source, job, human need, legitimate rights and owner-approved outreach permission. Human-in-the-loop is mandatory for external outreach and irreversible financial actions.

## Proof template
Audience; user job; actual valuable asset and fixture; exact prospective source publication; current relevance; permission and sponsor disclosure; real outreach action; created link status; referral session and actual task completion; cost; bias/unknowns; rollback/stop decision. **No local skill test can certify real link placement or traffic.**
