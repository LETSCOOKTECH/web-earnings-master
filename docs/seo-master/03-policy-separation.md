# 03 — Search, Google Ads and AdSense are different policies

## Google Search ranking/spam
Search Essentials and spam policies govern crawl/index eligibility, original people-first value, keyword stuffing, doorway abuse, scaled low-value pages, misleading tool functionality and link spam. Google ignores meta keywords. There is no official fixed keyword density, prescribed word count or ranking guarantee. A high-CPC keyword does not automatically rank or earn more revenue.

## Google Ads keyword research
Keyword Planner provides advertiser-side estimated monthly searches, competition, bids and campaign forecasts. The competition metric is **not organic SEO keyword difficulty**; advertiser CPC is **not AdSense publisher RPM**. Plan/account/geo and date matter. Never subscribe, buy keywords/ads or consume paid API quota without owner permission.

## Google AdSense publisher monetization
AdSense review requires unique useful relevant content and a real usable website. Google-served ads may be limited on no-content, low-value, under-construction or ad-heavy pages. Do not put ads where they resemble download, menu or primary controls; avoid accidental clicks, incentives, popups, invalid bots or purchased fake traffic. Rights/copyright, privacy/consent, ads.txt and account approval vary with actual property/country/account. Don't invent a magic 1000-word or 20-article approval threshold. A working utility can be eligible for search but still rejected by AdSense.

## Mandatory agent boundaries
Each indexable page delivers a real job and truthful proof; human-centered title/H1/description/anchors; no duplicate synonym or geography farms. Some error, loading, private-output and no-content screens should show **no ads**. Separate search eligibility, actual GSC indexing, actual publisher account approval, valid monetizable pageviews, and reconciled revenue. No local code audit authorizes ad publisher IDs.
