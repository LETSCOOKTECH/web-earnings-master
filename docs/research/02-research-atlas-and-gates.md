## Highest-value investigation order
**First: market and product truth.** M01, M02, M03, M05, C01, C03, C07, P01, P02, X03. Establish real problem, demand, nonbranded click opportunity and a measurable competitive advantage before programmatic page expansion.

**Second: runnable engineering and index correctness.** P03-P07, T01-T07, X01-X07. Benchmark formats, browser memory, output correctness, security, WAF, actual canonical/robots noindex and deployed server status.

**Third: global and AI-source behavior.** A01-A07 and S01-S07. Differentiate Google Search, Google AI Mode/Overviews, Gemini, ChatGPT Search, Claude, Perplexity, Bing/Copilot, regional search and browser agents. A user's direct file-conversion workflow does not require citations or pretend bots are users.

**Fourth: monetization and attributable economics.** R01-R07, V01-V07, L01-L07. Use current AdSense account status and policies; verify user value, ad density, consent, ads.txt, invalid traffic, revenue net of compute/egress and refunds. No fixed universal RPM, CTR or guaranteed account approval.

**Fifth: distribution, resilience and portfolio.** D01-D07, O01-O07, returning users and genuinely useful APIs/extensions/embeds, original shareworthy results and compliant outreach. Record actual traffic-channel contribution and controlled experiment uncertainty.

This order maximizes information value and minimizes wasting engineering work. It is not an imposed permanent order: modules can be selected manually by the user, and a fresh provider security/policy incident can override order.

## Evidence hierarchy
1. Current first-party controlling provider specs and exact applicable account/market rule.
2. Dated observed public pages, with vendor claims distinguished from independently tested functionality.
3. Licensed third-party modeled market, SEO and backlink signals, exact date/country/definition and uncertainty.
4. Real controlled browser/HTTP/task fixtures with reproducible outputs.
5. Authorized site accounts: Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, publisher panels, actual task analytics and server logs; no account means unknown.
6. Hypotheses and alternative causes retained distinctly until falsified/tested.

## Definitions that protect us from deceptive research
**Traffic** always states whether all-channel sessions, modeled Google organic visits, actual publisher clicks or bot requests. **Rank #1** always names query, date, locale, device and page URL. **Citation** never implies user click. **CPC-based traffic value** never implies AdSense earnings. **Indexed** never implies relevant traffic. **A URL exists** does not mean unique task or Google index. **Website quality** requires actual successful file/result output, not a promotional screenshot. **Tool simplicity** and **product scope** are validated through task metrics, not arbitrary page count.

## Absolute rejection criteria
False "100% rank" promises; fake/paid unnatural backlinks; programmatic pages differing only by search-keyword substitution; claims of proprietary AI citation weights; fabricated Ahrefs outputs; unverified AdSense status; misleading ads/download controls; copied competitor site text/code; unsafe upload-by-URL SSRF; excessive server work without quotas; output files unopenable or corrupted; no provenance or tests. See the Google Search spam policy and AdSense guidance.
