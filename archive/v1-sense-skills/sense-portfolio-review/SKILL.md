# Portfolio review

## Weekly/monthly cadence
1. Read each property's latest verified release and analytics reports; distinguish current site vs historical models.
2. Evaluate search visibility and real human visits by country and branded/non-branded; AI citations vs referrals separately.
3. Evaluate task success, processing error severity, usability feedback, return rate and current ad density/CWV.
4. Reconcile gross ad revenue, invalid traffic deductions, actual payout vs reported estimates, bandwidth/hosting/maintenance and net profitability.
5. Ask whether expansion increases human task completion and defensibility or just indexed URL count. New tool/page requires an independent researched user job.
6. Run policy-drift and ad-network-term checks. Escalate compliance or security blockers before growth experiments.
7. Decide FIX, TEST, SCALE, HOLD, or RETIRE. Record owner, decision evidence and next review.

## Never do
Do not equate high keyword volume with revenue, traffic estimates with internal analytics, ranking movement with a causal change, or high page counts with quality. Avoid domain farms and artificial cross-domain links.
