02 / UNDER THE HOOD

How duplicates are detected

No mystery rules. Each CSV record is parsed before comparison so commas inside quotes or multiline fields do not break the results.

01

Parse the records

Handles escaped quotes, quoted commas and multiline fields. Mismatched column counts are rejected instead of silently shifted.

02

Compare with control

Match entire records or one chosen column, with optional case-insensitive and whitespace-trimmed comparisons. The first record wins.

03

Review and export

See the kept and removed counts, preview up to eight rows, then export comma-separated data with CRLF line breaks.

03 / TRUST & LIMITS

Your data stays yours

The tool runs entirely in your browser with no upload endpoint, analytics or advertisement scripts. There is no user account or storage service.

What is supported?

UTF-8 comma-separated files only, up to 2 MB, 5,000 records and 200 columns. Excel XLSX, TSV, unsupported encodings, malformed quotes and rows with unequal column counts are not accepted.

What happens to the values?

Unless you enable one-column matching, the first occurrence of each complete row is retained. Whitespace trimming affects matching only; exported values are unchanged except the optional spreadsheet-formula guard. Leading apostrophe protection is not a guarantee against every spreadsheet or save/reopen behavior.
