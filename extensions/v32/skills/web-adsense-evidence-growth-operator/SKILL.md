# V32 — AdSense-First Website Business Operating System

**Invocation:** User requests deep research or execution for Google AdSense websites, organic traffic and AI-search visibility, from finding a product problem through real income and expansion. Follow this playbook to train an agent to **research and verify, not simply claim**. This is **an opt-in companion**, not a silently activated 51st registered V23 skill. Read root \`AGENTS.md\`, the existing 50-skill routing matrix and task-relevant specialists; do not weaken tests or use a single ad-focused outcome as an excuse to compromise user value.

**Purpose:** Sequence seven grounded execution stages with explicit ownership, verified official primary sources, independently reproducible fixtures and externally verified outcomes. A large source atlas is a research index, not an assertion of proprietary Google's ranking knowledge. No tool, source bundle, tutorial or agent can know literally every ranking signal or guarantee positions, traffic, page RPM, AdSense approval or net income. Treat success as *reported evidence of actual completed user tasks and lawful settled advertising revenue*.

## Stage 1. MARKET_DEMAND — choose a defensible task, not a fashionable niche

**Owner:** \`web-opportunity-intelligence\`, \`web-niche-validation\`, \`web-traffic-hunter\`. Classify the user's narrow human job, buying/learning moment, language, SERP country/device, usage frequency, urgency, alternatives (Google tools, calculators, search snippets, operating system, spreadsheet, browser, LLMs and competitors) and whether a click is necessary to complete the real job.

Research at least two *independent originating* evidence classes: a first-hand task complaint/interview/owned telemetry AND real search behavior from appropriately scoped Google Trends, Keyword Planner or owned GSC. Paid keyword platforms are optional additional models, not Google data. Verify long-tail query groups by intent; split informational queries from task-completion queries; avoid keyword variations generating thin repeated landing pages. Note task seasonal cycles, regional units, language, desktop/mobile and advertiser-facing brand safety. Publish an explicit **keyword-to-task coverage map** with each page's separate user benefit.

**False positives:** Trends 70 means 70 monthly searches; Keyword Planner advertiser competition LOW means Google organic low difficulty; Ahrefs KD under 10 guarantees first page; older domain wins automatically; US advertiser bid approximates publisher RPM; one screenshot is real volume. All are invalid. Do not buy shared analytics logins or bypass site authorization.

**Minimum outputs:** user-job, hypothesized task, raw source, observation date, scoped units, contradictory evidence, expected successful user output, competing AI substitution and 3 falsification options. A score only ranks *hypotheses*, never grants launch approval.

## Stage 2. COMPETITOR_GAP — prove current competing tools fail a real job

**Owner:** \`web-competitor-audit\` and \`web-product-validation\`. Observe search results as real users in a specified country/device/date. Capture page URLs, snapshots, titles, answer/AI block, directories, major incumbents, Google-owned widgets, mobile experience and ad density. Do not fabricate SERP positions from model recall. Assess public domain-registration/Wayback data as **proxy age only**; site birth and first ranking cannot be inferred from WHOIS alone.

Test **three different substitutes** with the **same normal, malformed and boundary input**. Record exact expected answer from an independent mathematical oracle, actual observed output, hidden caveats, accessibility/privacy, number of steps, error recovery, copy/download correctness and mobile constraints. Distinguish *advertised* feature from *verified tested* feature. Ask: Is a competitor weakness meaningful, repeatable and worth switching for? If no, stop. A novel color palette is not defensibility.

**AI substitution:** For each job compare what a browser extension, AI search, spreadsheet formula and public LLM can actually finish. Answer-only and one-line calculations tend to be weak; deterministic multi-step file-processing/export tasks may be differentiated but must pass competing-tool benchmarks. Do not assert tools are universally protected from Google's Canvas AI features.

**Minimum outputs:** real SERP receipts, 3+ competitor URLs and reproducible test logs, strongest competitor's success case, failure conditions, original value hypothesis, realistic distribution counterstrategy and third-party data limitations.

## Stage 3. PRODUCT_ORACLE — ship correct, usable, economic software

**Owner:** \`web-product-spec\`, \`web-astro-engineering\`, \`web-product-validation\`, \`web-security-performance\`, \`web-workload-architecture-router\`. Freeze a job-focused MVP scope and accepted non-goals. Prefer Astro static/SSR or another runtime based on requirements; do not assume any framework ranks better. For computation that can safely execute in browsers, avoid unnecessary server charges; bound browser memory/CPU and large inputs. Use backend isolation for truly server-required or confidential operations.

**QA must include:** independently derived mathematical output or golden files; valid, malformed, Unicode, encoding, boundary, empty, large-file and adversarial fixtures; zero information loss where promised; browser tests on Chromium/Firefox/WebKit and mobile; screen readers, keyboard, focus, contrast, responsive controls, errors, offline, download integrity and dark/light themes if offered. Security: input limits, CSP, CSRF/cookies if needed, server secrets, dependency review, SSRF for fetcher, sanitized CSV/HTML, upload privacy, cache TTL/retention, abuse/cost limits, backups and recovery if persistent. Enforce CI and measure runtime behavior rather than trusting generated UI.

A "10-minute AI-generated app" is at most a prototype until independently tested, accessible, secure, domain deployed, and a working user task. Tool function comes before ads. Lab good CWV does not ensure real users or ranking.

**Minimum outputs:** PRD, fixtures/oracles, version, tests, asset/bundle measures, privacy/consent, operational cost, known limitations, owner approval and rollback.

## Stage 4. SEARCH_DEPLOYMENT — separate crawl, index, rank and citations

**Owners:** \`web-technical-seo\`, \`web-ai-search-optimization\`, \`web-search-analytics\`, \`web-launch-review\`. Ground all technical changes in current Google Search Essentials, AI Search guidance, JavaScript SEO and Search Console.

- Produce real HTTP 200 canonical HTML for useful routes. Check CDN redirects, 404/soft-404, mobile parity, first HTML, JS rendering, status/caching, robots.txt, meta/X-Robots noindex, rel canonical, internal links, sitemap, hreflang when true, heading/title/snippet, structured data only when actually eligible, ads.txt when owner approved and HTTPS.
- Prevent programmatic doorway patterns: each route must execute distinct task value, avoid exact same form with a number swapped and duplicated SEO filler. Group interchangeable numerical transformations under one canonical job when doing otherwise adds no benefit. Publish original mathematical assumptions, worked examples, edge cases and sources where needed.
- Google's 2026 AI search guidance states no mandatory \`llms.txt\`, bespoke machine-readable AI markup or fan-out pages are required. Do not promise AI citations, and check live program-specific controls (e.g. Search Console setting) before declaring eligibility. Google generative AI visibility reports began limited rollout in June 2026; a site not seeing that report is not necessarily ineligible.
- Measure distinct events: crawl -> indexed -> impressions -> rank/query/page -> organic clicks -> fulfilled human tasks -> valid ad earnings. A site rank is SERP/geography/personalization specific. Site age is not a launch criterion.
- P75 field CWV good targets in applicable reports, plus browser lab. No fake "CLS 0.000 guaranteed", "SEO 100/100 dominates", or fixed keyword-density requirements.

**Minimum outputs:** production domain and actual allowed owner inspection, independent crawl/index evidence, Search Console connected receipt, technical reports, verified URL metrics and status \`UNKNOWN_EXTERNAL\` until deployed.

## Stage 5. REAL_DISTRIBUTION — earn genuine users, not impressions

**Owner:** \`web-traffic-hunter\`, \`web-portfolio-growth\`. Prioritize searchable original resources, useful comparisons, high-value tutorials, community answers without spam, integrations, earned references and repeat-friendly UX. Make referral links truthful, respect nofollow/sponsored for paid relationships, editorial independence, robots and subreddit/forum rules. Track campaign UTMs without leaking sensitive private data and distinguish email/organic/direct/referrals by analytics. Monitor channels for anomalous click patterns, bot/referrer spam and unnatural impressions; stop risky sources.

**Forbidden:** bought auto-surf/paid-to-click exchange, proxies to fabricate high-RPM geos, deceptive accounts/Gmail farms, traffic laundering through search/affiliate pages, forced ad interaction, intentional ad click incentives, manufactured rankings/link networks or automated engagements. Do not disguise bad acquisition as a "growth hack." Legitimate paid advertising may be legal but its *actual cost* and traffic quality must be reconciled, not assumed profitable on AdSense.

**Minimum outputs:** actual channel, permission, content and user-task alignment, Search Console/GSC vs analytics reconciliation, fulfilled tasks, acquisition cost, cohorts/return rate and safeguards. A raw view counter is not a human or valid ad impression.

## Stage 6. ADSENSE_SITE_ACCOUNT — real approval and ad placement

**Owner:** \`web-adsense-readiness\`, \`web-publisher-policy-eligibility\`, \`web-ad-experience\`, \`web-privacy-consent\`. Eligibility: ownership and original value, AdSense publisher account rules (one account per publisher), age and supported geography/language, contact/legal/privacy as genuinely applicable, actual helpful tool UX; not a magic 30 articles, domain age or minimum monthly views. Apply through actual Google Sites workflow; ownership verification -> policy review -> real **Ready**. Do not interpret site verification, ads.txt or a passing static code check as approval.

Only install real code for approved site/account when owner provides credentials via a safe path. Avoid ads adjacent to upload/convert/download, content mimics, accidental clicks, incentives, ads on nonpublisher-content screens, automatic AdSense refresh, deceptive gates or interrupted user tasks. If EEA/UK/Switzerland audience, check certified TCF CMP with currently applicable version and consent behavior. Test functional pages with AdBlock and script failures without bypassing users' blocker preferences. Avoid stating hardcoded 32px is a universal Google policy; measure actual accidental click risk.

**Minimum outputs:** Google Ready evidence only from authorized account, original current policy citations, actual ad placement screenshots in live browser, legal/consent review, ads.txt with actual seller ID, page UX measures, invalid-traffic monitoring and rollback.

## Stage 7. RECONCILED_ECONOMICS — expand only profitably

**Owner:** \`web-unit-economics-finops\`, \`web-search-analytics\` and portfolio specialist. Keep *advertiser CPC* separate from *publisher page RPM*. Normalize real period, geo, device, page, session, pageview, ad request/impression and valid ad earnings. Google page RPM = estimated earnings / pageviews * 1000. Only an owner-authorized AdSense account contains its actual report. Estimated earnings may be adjusted; payout cash/fees, currency/FX, taxes and cost ledger determine net contribution, not estimated screenshots.

Evaluate 3 plausible \`--scenario\` cases with explicitly labeled **hypothetical assumptions**, including seasonal/geo RPM uncertainty, invalid deductions, revenue decline, slow SEO, higher cost, abuse and paid API consumption. Never output plausible CPM as though the market or network promised it. Run controlled improvements only after baseline: product task success, navigation, original editorial value, legitimate shareability, structured outputs and ad placement without degrading interaction. Track regression and Google updates; stop or pivot when actual effort/return invalidates the hypothesis.

**Minimum outputs:** actual site and month, observed pageviews, owner AdSense report and payout evidence, cost invoices, valid revenue, quality/retention metrics, risk, rollback and explicit \`CONTINUE / IMPROVE / PAUSE\` backed by accountable owner decision.

A valid JSON packet is **STRUCTURAL_ONLY**; programmatically invented account "Ready" status cannot be authenticated. The tool outputs all stage blockers and refuses to approve any business automatically. An operator still must inspect real evidence and provider dashboard, and must not promote a self-authored fixture to a business proof. If a stage fails, stop and explicitly report the gap; moving an agent into the next stage because a user urges "100%" is not allowed. \`sources/adsense_growth_primary_atlas_v32.json\` source snapshot dates are not perpetual proof: action-time official rechecks are mandatory.

## Required answer pattern for real tasks

Report: (1) precise user task and geographic/tech market, (2) researched demand with metric provenance, (3) competitor same-input failures *and counterexamples*, (4) AI substitution and what only a real website completes, (5) original product advantage and independent QA, (6) crawl/index/AI eligibility, (7) real non-manipulated distribution, (8) AdSense readiness and consent, (9) scenario versus actual earnings, (10) stage gate, unknowns and **single next falsification test**. References and a big list of policies without actual verified task work do not equal execution completion.

**Never claim**: complete global niche database, instant approval, Google ranking dominance, 100× traffic, guaranteed organic hits, 95% fill, a fixed "minimum 7 months domain age", AI citations by adding special files, multi-account AdSense ban evasion, or unlimited monetizable traffic from an agent.

**Explicit operating rules:** Astro is not a Google ranking guarantee. Maintain the AdSense site/account approval gate as independent owner evidence. No automatic background monitoring, account enrollment, paid spending, or webmaster traffic generation without explicit user authorization.
